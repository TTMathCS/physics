# Start here: first lessons

*Six first lessons for young beginners. Pick any one, or go in order. Each lesson ends with where it leads; when you like a topic, climb its ladder of 8 levels.*

| | Lesson | The question | Its topic |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Why things fall down](topics/gravity/level-1/01-things-fall-down.md) | where is "down"? | [Gravity and orbits](topics/gravity/index.md) |
| 2 | [Jumping on Mars](topics/gravity/level-1/05-jumping-on-mars.md) | how high could you jump there? | [Gravity and orbits](topics/gravity/index.md) |
| 3 | [The Moon goes round](topics/gravity/level-1/06-the-moon-goes-round.md) | why does it not fall? | [Gravity and orbits](topics/gravity/index.md) |
| 4 | [Pushes make things go](topics/rockets/level-1/01-pushes-make-things-go.md) | what starts a thing moving? | [Rockets and landing](topics/rockets/index.md) |
| 5 | [The balloon rocket](topics/rockets/level-1/02-the-balloon-rocket.md) | how does a balloon fly? | [Rockets and landing](topics/rockets/index.md) |
| 6 | [Coming down gently](topics/rockets/level-1/05-coming-down-gently.md) | how do you land softly? | [Rockets and landing](topics/rockets/index.md) |

There are 10 topics in all: see [every topic](topics/index.md).

For parents and teachers: level 1 suits most children from about 5, level 3 from about 9, level 5 from about 12, level 7 from about 15. Every lesson works with things from home, and the answers to the questions are folded until you tap them.
