# Level 8: Gravity and time

*Space and time · level 8 of 8 · rough age 17 and beyond (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

Einstein's general relativity says gravity is not a force pulling through space, but the shape of space and time
itself. This level follows the evidence: falling, clocks at different heights, the GPS satellites, starlight bent by
the Sun, radio signals from Mars arriving late, and finally the clock a Mars base would need.
