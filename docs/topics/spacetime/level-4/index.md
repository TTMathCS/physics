# Level 4: Clocks on Mars

*Space and time · level 4 of 8 · rough age 11-12 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

A day on Mars is 39 minutes longer than ours. A year is nearly twice as long, and its seasons are not even the same
length. This level builds a clock and a calendar for Mars, the way mission teams actually keep time there.
