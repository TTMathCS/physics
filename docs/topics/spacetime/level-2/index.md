# Level 2: Near and far

*Space and time · level 2 of 8 · rough age 7-8 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

How big is the Earth, and how far away are the Moon, the Sun and Mars? This level measures them in a way you can
picture: how long a car would take to get there, and how many Earths would fit in between. It ends by shrinking the
whole solar system down to fit in a park.
