# Level 6: Light delay

*Space and time · level 6 of 8 · rough age 13-14 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

Every message to Mars is minutes old when it arrives, and the delay changes from day to day. This level works out the
delay from the two orbits, then follows how mission teams live with it: one plan a sol, relays in orbit, two quiet weeks
when the Sun gets in the way, and radio timing so precise it measures the distance to Mars.
