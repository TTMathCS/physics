# Level 1: What makes things go

*Energy and power · level 1 of 8 · rough age 5-6 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

In this level you find out what energy is: what it takes to make things move, warm up, light up or grow. You meet the
energy in food, in sunlight, in batteries and in the wind, and see what a home on Mars would need energy for, and where
it could get it.

| Lesson | You will be able to |
| --- | --- |
| [Energy makes things go](01-energy-makes-things-go.md) | Say what energy is used for, and spot things that are using it |
| [Energy from food](02-energy-from-food.md) | Explain that food gives your body the energy to move, grow and keep warm |
| [Energy from the Sun](03-energy-from-the-sun.md) | Explain that sunlight brings energy, and that it is weaker on Mars |
| [Batteries](04-batteries.md) | Explain that a battery stores energy until it is needed |
| [Wind and water](05-wind-and-water.md) | Explain that moving air and water carry energy, and why Mars wind is weak |
| [Energy on Mars](06-energy-on-mars.md) | List what a Mars home needs energy for, and where it could come from |
