# Energy changes form

*Energy and power · level 2, lesson 4 of 6*

## A question to start

You eat breakfast, run up a hill, and slide down the other side on a sledge. Can you follow the energy all the way, from
your plate to the bottom of the hill?

## The idea

Energy comes in many forms:

| Form | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Kinetic (moving) | A rolling ball, the wind |
| Gravitational (height) | A book on a high shelf |
| Elastic (stretched) | A pulled-back bow |
| Chemical (in substances) | Food, batteries, fuel |
| Thermal (heat) | A hot cup of cocoa |
| Light | Sunshine, a lamp |
| Sound | A drum, a voice |
| Electrical | Electricity in a wire |

Energy can **change** from one form into another. Almost everything that happens is energy changing form.

When energy changes form, some always turns into **heat** along the way. Your muscles get warm when you run. The sledge's
runners warm the snow a little as they rub.

**On Mars**, the energy chain of a solar-powered rover goes: light from the Sun → electrical energy from the panels →
chemical energy stored in the battery → electrical energy again → kinetic energy as the wheels turn, and some heat.

## Worked examples

**1. A torch.** Chemical (battery) → electrical (wire) → light, and some heat (the bulb gets warm).

**2. A wind turbine.** Kinetic (wind) → kinetic (spinning blades) → electrical.

**3. A firework.** Chemical → light, sound, heat and kinetic.

## Try it

1. What form of energy is stored in food?
2. What energy change happens in a solar panel?
3. Follow the energy in a toy car with a battery.
4. Why do your hands get warm when you clap?

??? note "Answers"
    1. Chemical energy.
    2. Light → electrical.
    3. Chemical (battery) → electrical → kinetic (moving), and some heat and sound.
    4. Some of the moving energy of your hands turns into heat (and sound) when they hit.

## Common mistake

**"Energy gets used up and disappears."** Energy never disappears. It only changes form, and in the end most of it
becomes heat that spreads out into the surroundings.
