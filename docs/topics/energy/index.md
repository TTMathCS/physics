# Energy and power

A city on Mars runs on energy: to heat its rooms, grow its food, split water and run its machines. This topic
starts with what makes things go and climbs to work, power, efficiency and the real choices for Mars: sunlight that
is less than half as strong as at Earth, dust on the panels, batteries, and small nuclear reactors.
