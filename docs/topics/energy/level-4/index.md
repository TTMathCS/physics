# Level 4: Joules and watts

*Energy and power · level 4 of 8 · rough age 11-12 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

In this level you measure energy and power. You meet the joule and the watt, change between joules and the
kilowatt-hours on an electricity bill, read the energy on a food label, work out the power of your own legs climbing
stairs on Earth and on Mars, and compare the power of a Mars rover with a home on Earth.

| Lesson | You will be able to |
| --- | --- |
| [The joule](01-the-joule.md) | Use the joule as the unit of energy, and feel how big one is on Mars and on Earth |
| [Power and the watt](02-the-watt.md) | Find power from energy and time, and give typical powers of everyday things |
| [Kilowatt-hours](03-kilowatt-hours.md) | Change between kilowatt-hours and joules, and work out what an appliance uses |
| [Energy in food](04-energy-in-food.md) | Read food labels in kJ and kcal, and find the power your body runs at |
| [The power of a person](05-power-of-a-person.md) | Work out your power climbing stairs, on Earth and on Mars |
| [Rovers and their power](06-rovers-and-power.md) | Compare the energy a rover has each sol with what a home uses each day |
