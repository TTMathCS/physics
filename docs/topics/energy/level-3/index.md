# Level 3: Energy in, energy out

*Energy and power · level 3 of 8 · rough age 9-10 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

In this level you meet the most important rule about energy: it is never made or destroyed, only changed from one form
into another. You follow energy along chains, find out where the "wasted" energy goes, watch it swap back and forth in
a pendulum and a bouncing ball, and see why a home on Mars would try never to waste any.

| Lesson | You will be able to |
| --- | --- |
| [Nothing is lost](01-nothing-is-lost.md) | State that energy is conserved, and check that the energy going in equals the energy coming out |
| [Energy chains](02-energy-chains.md) | Draw an energy chain from the Sun to a moving rover |
| [Where the waste goes](03-where-the-waste-goes.md) | Tell useful energy from wasted energy, and say where the waste goes |
| [The swinging pendulum](04-pendulum.md) | Explain the energy changes in a pendulum, and why it swings more slowly on Mars |
| [The bouncing ball](05-bouncing-ball.md) | Explain why each bounce is lower than the last |
| [Saving energy](06-saving-energy.md) | Give ways to waste less energy, at home and in a Mars habitat |
