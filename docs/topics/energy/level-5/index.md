# Level 5: Work and kinetic energy

*Energy and power · level 5 of 8 · rough age 12-13 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

In this level energy gets its formulas. You meet work, $W = Fd$, the way a force passes energy on; kinetic energy,
$\tfrac12 mv^2$; and gravitational potential energy, $mgh$. You use them to find how fast a dropped spanner hits the
ground on Mars and on Earth, and how far a moving cart slides before friction stops it.

| [Work at an angle](02-work-at-an-angle.md) | Use $W = Fd\cos\theta$ for a force at an angle to the motion |
| [Kinetic energy](03-kinetic-energy.md) | Use $E_k = \tfrac12 mv^2$, and see where it comes from |

| [The work-energy theorem](06-work-energy-theorem.md) | Use net work = change in kinetic energy to find stopping distances |
