# Kinetic energy

*Energy and power · level 5, lesson 3 of 6*

## A question to start

Perseverance weighs a tonne but creeps along at 4 cm/s. A pebble from space hits Mars at 10 km/s. Which carries more
energy?

## The idea

The **kinetic energy** of a mass $m$ moving at speed $v$ is

$$E_k = \tfrac12 m v^2.$$

Where does it come from? Push a mass $m$ from rest with a steady force $F$ over a distance $d$. The work done is $Fd$.
The acceleration is $a = F/m$, and from the motion equations $v^2 = 2ad$. So

$$W = Fd = (ma)\left(\frac{v^2}{2a}\right) = \tfrac12 mv^2.$$

The work done on it becomes its kinetic energy. Because of the $v^2$, speed matters far more than mass: double the
speed, four times the energy.

| Moving thing | Mass | Speed | Kinetic energy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Perseverance at top speed | 1,025 kg | 0.042 m/s | 0.9 J |
| A person walking | 70 kg | 1.4 m/s | 69 J |
| A car on a motorway | 1,500 kg | 30 m/s | 675 kJ |
| A 1 kg rock hitting Mars from space | 1 kg | 10 km/s | 50 MJ |

Kinetic energy does **not** depend on gravity: a 10 kg cart at 2 m/s has 20 J on Mars or on Earth. A collision on Mars at
the same speed is just as hard as on Earth.

## Worked examples

**1. A walker.** $\tfrac12 \times 70 \times 1.4^2 = 68.6$ J.

**2. A meteorite.** A 1 kg rock at 10,000 m/s: $\tfrac12 \times 1 \times 10^8 = 5\times10^7$ J $= 50$ MJ, about the
energy in a litre and a half of petrol, released in an instant.

**3. Finding a speed.** A 0.5 kg ball has 9 J of kinetic energy. $v = \sqrt{2E/m} = \sqrt{36} = 6$ m/s.

## Try it

1. Find the kinetic energy of a 2 kg box sliding at 3 m/s.
2. A buggy of 800 kg drives at 5 m/s on Mars. Find its kinetic energy.
3. The buggy speeds up to 10 m/s. By what factor does its kinetic energy grow?
4. What speed gives a 60 kg runner 3,000 J of kinetic energy?

??? note "Answers"
    1. $\tfrac12 \times 2 \times 9 = 9$ J.
    2. $\tfrac12 \times 800 \times 25 = 10{,}000$ J.
    3. Four times: 40,000 J.
    4. $v = \sqrt{2 \times 3000/60} = \sqrt{100} = 10$ m/s.

## Common mistake

**Forgetting to square the speed.** $\tfrac12 mv^2$, not $\tfrac12 mv$. Check units: kg × (m/s)² = kg m²/s² = J.
