# Level 6: Efficiency and the Sun

*Energy and power · level 6 of 8 · rough age 13-14 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

In this level you put a number on waste, the efficiency, and draw it as a Sankey diagram. Then you turn to the biggest
energy source a Mars city has: the Sun. You work out how much weaker sunlight is at Mars, how big a solar panel must be,
and what dust on the panels and dust in the sky do to the power, as the rovers Spirit, Opportunity and the lander
InSight found out.
