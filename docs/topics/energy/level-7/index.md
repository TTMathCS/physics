# Level 7: Storing energy

*Energy and power · level 7 of 8 · rough age 15-16 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

Sunlight comes by day and stops at night, and on Mars a dust storm can dim it for months. This level is about keeping
energy for later: in batteries, in hydrogen, in spinning wheels and in raised weights. You compare them by energy per
kilogram and by how much comes back out, then size a store to carry a base through a storm.
