# Level 4: Speed and direction

*Forces and motion · level 4 of 8 · rough age 11-12 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

In this level you put numbers on motion. You work out speeds and change between metres per second and kilometres per
hour, tell speed from velocity, read distance-time graphs, and meet acceleration, the rate at which speed changes. You
see why going round a corner always needs a force, and plan a long drive across Mars with a rover's real speed.

| Lesson | You will be able to |
| --- | --- |
| [How fast?](01-speed.md) | Work out a speed from a distance and a time, in m/s and km/h |
| [Speed and velocity](02-velocity.md) | Explain the difference between speed and velocity |
| [Distance-time graphs](03-distance-time-graphs.md) | Read speeds and stops from a distance-time graph |
| [Speeding up and slowing down](04-acceleration.md) | Work out an acceleration and read a speed-time graph |
| [Turning needs a force](05-turning-needs-a-force.md) | Explain why anything that changes direction needs a force, and where it comes from |
| [Planning a long drive](06-planning-a-long-drive.md) | Use average speed to plan how many sols a rover drive will take |
