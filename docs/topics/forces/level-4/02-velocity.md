# Speed and velocity

*Forces and motion · level 4, lesson 2 of 6*

## A question to start

Two rovers drive at 4 cm/s. One heads north, the other south. Are they doing the same thing? And a toy train goes round
a circular track at a steady speed. Is anything about its motion changing?

## The idea

**Speed** says how fast. **Velocity** says how fast **and which way**. 4 cm/s is a speed; 4 cm/s north is a velocity.

That makes velocity something with a direction, like a force, and we draw it with an arrow. Two rovers at 4 cm/s, one
going north and one south, have the same speed but **opposite velocities**.

The toy train on the circle keeps the same speed, but its direction keeps changing. So its **velocity is changing**
all the time, even though its speed is not.

Velocities going opposite ways along a line can be written with signs: say east is positive, then 5 m/s east is
$+5$ m/s and 5 m/s west is $-5$ m/s.

Why care? Because forces change **velocity**, not just speed. A force is needed to change how fast you go, **or** which
way. That is why the next lessons say acceleration and turning both need a force.

## Worked examples

**1. A ball thrown up.** At 10 m/s upwards, then at the top 0 m/s, then on the way down 10 m/s downwards. Same speed at
start and end, opposite velocities.

**2. A round trip.** A rover drives 50 m east and then 50 m back west in 40 minutes. Its average speed is
$100 \text{ m} \div 40 \text{ min} = 2.5$ m/min. But it ended where it started, so its average velocity is zero.

## Try it

1. What is the difference between speed and velocity?
2. A car drives round a roundabout at a steady 20 km/h. Is its velocity steady?
3. With east as positive, write 3 m/s west as a velocity.
4. A runner does one lap of a 400 m track in 80 s. What is her average speed? Her average velocity?

??? note "Answers"
    1. Velocity is speed with a direction.
    2. No: its direction keeps changing.
    3. $-3$ m/s.
    4. Average speed $400 \div 80 = 5$ m/s. Average velocity zero: she ends where she began.

## Common mistake

**"Constant speed means constant velocity."** Only in a straight line. Anything going round a curve at a steady speed has
a changing velocity.
