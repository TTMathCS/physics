# Speeding up and slowing down

*Forces and motion · level 4, lesson 4 of 6*

## A question to start

Drop a stone. After one second it is falling at about 10 m/s on Earth. After two seconds, 20 m/s. On Mars, after one
second only about 3.7 m/s. How do we describe how quickly something gains speed?

## The idea

**Acceleration** is how fast the velocity changes:

$$\text{acceleration} = \frac{\text{change in velocity}}{\text{time taken}}.$$

Velocity in m/s, divided by time in s, gives acceleration in **metres per second per second**, written **m/s²**.

- A car going from 0 to 20 m/s in 10 s accelerates at $20 \div 10 = 2$ m/s²: it gains 2 m/s every second.
- Slowing down is acceleration too, with a minus sign, sometimes called **deceleration**.

A falling thing, with no air in the way, accelerates at a steady rate called **g**:

| Free fall | Mars | Earth |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Acceleration, g | 3.71 m/s² | 9.81 m/s² |
| Speed after 1 s | 3.7 m/s | 9.8 m/s |
| Speed after 3 s | 11.1 m/s | 29.4 m/s |

A **speed-time graph** shows this. A straight sloping line means a steady acceleration, and its slope is the
acceleration. A flat line means a steady speed, no acceleration.

## Worked examples

**1. A train.** From 30 m/s to 0 in 60 s: $(0 - 30) \div 60 = -0.5$ m/s². It loses 0.5 m/s every second.

**2. A Mars lander's last seconds.** Falling at 30 m/s, its rockets slow it to 2 m/s in 14 s:
$(2 - 30) \div 14 = -2$ m/s².

**3. How long to reach a speed.** A stone dropped on Mars: how long to reach 15 m/s? $15 \div 3.71 = 4.0$ s.

## Try it

1. A bike goes from 2 m/s to 8 m/s in 3 s. What is its acceleration?
2. A sprinter reaches 10 m/s in 2.5 s from a standing start. What is her acceleration?
3. How fast is a stone falling on Mars after 5 s, with no air?
4. On a speed-time graph, what does a flat line mean?

??? note "Answers"
    1. $(8 - 2) \div 3 = 2$ m/s².
    2. $10 \div 2.5 = 4$ m/s².
    3. $5 \times 3.71 = 18.6$ m/s.
    4. A steady speed: no acceleration.

## Common mistake

**"Acceleration means going fast."** Something can go very fast with no acceleration at all, like a spacecraft coasting
at 30 km/s. Acceleration means the velocity is **changing**.
