# Level 8: Rotation and stress

*Forces and motion · level 8 of 8 · rough age 17 and beyond (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

In this level forces make things turn and stretch. You meet torque, moment of inertia and the turning form of Newton's
second law, use the conservation of angular momentum on a skater, a spinning habitat and the planet Mars itself, and
learn the language of stress and strain. It ends with a surprise for anyone building on Mars: the biggest load on a
habitat is not its weight but the air inside it.

| Lesson | You will be able to |
| --- | --- |
| [Torque](01-torque.md) | Find the turning effect of a force, and explain why undoing a bolt is harder on Mars |
| [Moment of inertia](02-moment-of-inertia.md) | Find moments of inertia and use them to race rolling objects down a slope |
| [Turning faster: τ = Iα](03-angular-acceleration.md) | Use the rotational second law for wheels and flywheels |
| [Angular momentum](04-angular-momentum.md) | Use conservation of angular momentum, from skaters to the spin of Mars |
| [Stress and strain](05-stress-and-strain.md) | Use stress, strain and Young's modulus to size a cable or a column |
| [Building under 0.38 g](06-building-under-0-38-g.md) | Compare the weight loads and pressure loads on a Mars habitat |
