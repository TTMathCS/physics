# Level 5: Newton's laws

*Forces and motion · level 5 of 8 · rough age 12-13 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

In this level you meet the three laws Isaac Newton published in 1687, which explain almost all the motion you will ever
see. You learn what inertia is and why mass measures it, use $F = ma$ and $W = mg$ on Mars and on Earth, find the
pairs of forces in the third law, and draw free-body diagrams, the tool every engineer uses to solve a force problem.

| Lesson | You will be able to |
| --- | --- |
| [The first law](01-first-law.md) | State Newton's first law and use it to explain seat belts and coasting spacecraft |
| [Mass and inertia](02-mass-and-inertia.md) | Explain why mass measures inertia, and how astronauts weigh themselves in orbit |
| [The second law, F = ma](03-second-law.md) | Use $F = ma$ to find a force, a mass or an acceleration |
| [Weight is a force, W = mg](04-weight.md) | Find weights on Mars and Earth, and explain why everything falls at the same rate |
| [The third law](05-third-law.md) | Find the pair of forces in any interaction, and say which objects they act on |
| [Free-body diagrams](06-free-body-diagrams.md) | Draw the forces on one object and use them with $F = ma$ |
