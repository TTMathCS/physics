# Level 3: Balanced and unbalanced

*Forces and motion · level 3 of 8 · rough age 9-10 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

In this level you learn to draw forces as arrows and to add them up. When the forces on something balance, it keeps
doing what it was doing; when they don't, it speeds up, slows down or turns. You meet the newton, the unit of force,
and the spring that measures it, and find out why a bathroom scale would tell a lie on Mars.
