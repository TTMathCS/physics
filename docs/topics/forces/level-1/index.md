# Level 1: Pushes and pulls

*Forces and motion · level 1 of 8 · rough age 5-6 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

In this level you find out what a force is: a push or a pull. You see that a force can start something moving, stop
it, speed it up or turn it, and squash or stretch it. You find out why heavy things are hard to get moving, and why on
Mars a box is easier to lift but still just as hard to push into motion.

| --- | --- |
| [Pushes and pulls](01-pushes-and-pulls.md) | Say whether an everyday action is a push or a pull |
| [Starting and stopping](02-starting-and-stopping.md) | Explain that a push or a pull is needed to start something moving and to stop it |
| [A bigger push](03-a-bigger-push.md) | Say what a bigger push does to how fast something starts moving |
| [Heavy things are harder to move](04-heavy-things.md) | Explain why a full trolley needs a bigger push than an empty one |
| [Turning and squashing](05-turning-and-squashing.md) | Name two more things a force can do: change the way something goes, and change its shape |
| [Moving things on Mars](06-moving-things-on-mars.md) | Explain why a box on Mars is easier to lift but just as hard to get moving |
