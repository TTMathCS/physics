# Moving things on Mars

*Forces and motion · level 1, lesson 6 of 6*

## A question to start

On Mars, gravity pulls much less than on Earth. A box that feels heavy here would feel light there. So would it be
easy to push a big box across the floor of a Mars home?

## The idea

There are two different jobs:

- **Lifting** a box means pulling it up against gravity. Gravity on Mars pulls only a little more than a third as
  hard as on Earth. So lifting is much easier on Mars.
- **Getting it moving** sideways, or stopping it, depends on how much stuff is in the box: its mass. The box has
  exactly the same mass on Mars as on Earth. So on smooth wheels, it is **just as hard to get going**, and just as hard
  to stop.

| | Mars | Earth |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How hard gravity pulls | a bit more than a third | all of it |
| A box that weighs 30 kg on Earth feels like | about 11 kg | 30 kg |
| Its mass, the stuff in it | 30 kg | 30 kg |
| How hard to get it rolling on wheels | the same | the same |

Sliding a box along the floor, without wheels, is a bit different. Then the floor rubs on the box, and a lighter box
presses down less, so it rubs less. That is easier on Mars.

## Worked examples

**1. A cart full of rocks.** An astronaut pulls a cart of rock samples. Getting it rolling takes the same pull as on
Earth, and once it is rolling it is just as hard to stop. Running into a full cart would hurt just as much.

**2. Lifting a toolbox.** A toolbox that is hard to lift on Earth could be lifted with one hand on Mars.

## Try it

1. Is lifting a box easier on Mars or on Earth?
2. Does a box have more mass, less, or the same on Mars?
3. Is it easier to get a box on wheels rolling on Mars?
4. A runaway cart rolls towards you on Mars. Is it easier to stop than on Earth?

??? note "Answers"
    1. On Mars: gravity pulls less.
    2. The same.
    3. No, just as hard: that depends on its mass.
    4. No, just as hard (if it is going as fast).

## Common mistake

**"On Mars everything is lighter, so everything is easier."** Only the jobs that fight gravity, like lifting and
climbing, are easier. Starting and stopping things depends on mass, which does not change.
