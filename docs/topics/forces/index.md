# Forces and motion

Everything that starts, stops, turns or speeds up does so because of a force. This topic starts with pushing toys
across the floor and climbs to Newton's three laws, friction, and the forces that hold a rover on a slope or a
habitat's spinning wheel together. On Mars the same laws hold; only the numbers change.

8 levels. The ages are only a rough guide for parents and teachers; anyone can start at any level.

| Level | Rough age | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Level 1: Pushes and pulls | 5-6 | Pushing, pulling, starting and stopping; heavy things are harder to move |
| Level 2: Rubbing and rolling | 7-8 | Friction, wheels, slopes, why a rover drives slowly on sand |
| Level 3: Balanced and unbalanced | 9-10 | Force arrows, balanced forces, the newton, springs that measure force |
| Level 4: Speed and direction | 11-12 | Speed, velocity, distance-time graphs, why turning needs a force |
| Level 5: Newton's laws | 12-13 | Inertia, F = ma, action and reaction, free-body diagrams |
| Level 6: Friction and slopes | 13-14 | Coefficients of friction, forces on a ramp, a rover parked on a 30° slope on Mars |
| Level 7: Moving in circles | 15-16 | Centripetal force, banked tracks, spinning a habitat for artificial gravity |
| Level 8: Rotation and stress | 17 and beyond | Torque, moments of inertia, angular momentum, the strength of materials under 0.38 g |
