# Forces and motion

Everything that starts, stops, turns or speeds up does so because of a force. This topic starts with pushing toys
across the floor and climbs to Newton's three laws, friction, and the forces that hold a rover on a slope or a
habitat's spinning wheel together. On Mars the same laws hold; only the numbers change.
