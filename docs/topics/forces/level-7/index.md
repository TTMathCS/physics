# Level 7: Moving in circles

*Forces and motion · level 7 of 8 · rough age 15-16 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

In this level you work out the acceleration of anything moving in a circle, $v^2/r$, and the force that has to supply
it. Then you use it on Mars, where weak gravity means less grip: how fast a vehicle can take a bend, how a banked track
helps, when a buggy leaves the ground over a bump, and how a spinning room could give people on Mars their full
Earth weight back.
