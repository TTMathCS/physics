# Level 5: Pressure and buoyancy

*Air and fluids · level 5 of 8 · rough age 12-13 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

This level puts the push of fluids into equations. You see how pressure spreads through a liquid in a hydraulic jack,
how it grows with depth, and why it grows more slowly on Mars. Then you measure upthrust with Archimedes' principle, use
density to say what floats and how deep, and size a balloon that could fly in Mars's thin air.
