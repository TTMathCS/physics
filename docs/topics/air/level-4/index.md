# Level 4: Thin air

*Air and fluids · level 4 of 8 · rough age 11-12 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

Just how thin is the air on Mars? This level puts numbers on it: how dense it is, what it is made of, and how it thins
out with height. Then it asks what thin air means for people: why there is nothing to breathe, why you need a pressure
suit, and why a Mars gale pushes like a breeze.
