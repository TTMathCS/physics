# Level 8: Winds of a planet

*Air and fluids · level 8 of 8 · rough age 17 and beyond (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

The last level scales up from a rotor blade to a whole planet. You start with the Reynolds number, which says when
viscosity matters and when flow turns turbulent, and find that Ingenuity's blades flew in air that behaves like syrup.
Then you build up the physics of weather: winds driven by pressure differences, turned by the planet's spin, carried
round in great circulations, and on Mars, whipped into dust storms that can cover the planet.
