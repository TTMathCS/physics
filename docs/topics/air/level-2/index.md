# Level 2: Floating and sinking

*Air and fluids · level 2 of 8 · rough age 7-8 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

Why does a huge ship float when a tiny pebble sinks? This level tests things in water, feels water pushing up, and finds
out why being heavy for your size matters more than being heavy. Then it turns to air, which pushes up too, and ends
with a boat and a balloon on Mars.

| Lesson | You will be able to |
| --- | --- |
| [Float or sink?](01-float-or-sink.md) | Test things in water and sort them into floaters and sinkers |
| [Water pushes up](02-water-pushes-up.md) | Feel and explain the upward push of water |
| [Heavy for its size](03-heavy-for-its-size.md) | Say why some things float and others the same size sink |
| [Boats](04-boats.md) | Explain how a boat made of heavy stuff can float |
| [Balloons](05-balloons.md) | Explain why some balloons float up in air |
| [Floating on Mars](06-floating-on-mars.md) | Say how floating would change on Mars, and how it would not |
