# Level 1: Wind and bubbles

*Air and fluids · level 1 of 8 · rough age 5-6 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

You cannot see air, but it is real stuff all around you. This first level shows how to tell it is there, what wind is,
why bubbles hold air, and how wind pushes kites and sails. At the end you meet the thin, dusty air of Mars and its
spinning dust devils.

| Lesson | You will be able to |
| --- | --- |
| [Air is all around](01-air-is-all-around.md) | Show that air is there, even when a cup looks empty |
| [Wind](02-wind.md) | Say what wind is and spot it moving things |
| [Blowing bubbles](03-blowing-bubbles.md) | Say what is inside a bubble and why it pops |
| [Wind pushes](04-wind-pushes.md) | Explain how wind pushes kites, sails and windmills |
| [Air to breathe](05-air-to-breathe.md) | Say which part of the air we need to breathe |
| [Wind on Mars](06-wind-on-mars.md) | Compare Mars's thin, dusty wind with Earth's |
