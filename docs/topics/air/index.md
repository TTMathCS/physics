# Air and fluids

Mars has about a sixtieth as much air above each square metre as Earth, and at the ground it is about 75 times less
dense. Yet that thin air carries dust storms around the planet, spins up dust devils, and held up a small helicopter for
72 flights. This topic starts with wind and bubbles and climbs to pressure, buoyancy, lift and drag, and the winds of a
whole planet.
