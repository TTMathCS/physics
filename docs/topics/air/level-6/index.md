# Level 6: Flowing fluids

*Air and fluids · level 6 of 8 · rough age 13-14 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

Now the fluids start moving. This level measures how much flows through a pipe, why a narrow nozzle makes water squirt
faster, and Bernoulli's principle: fast flow, low pressure. You use it to measure wind speed, to see how a wing lifts,
and to work out how fast Ingenuity's rotor blades swept through Mars's thin air.
