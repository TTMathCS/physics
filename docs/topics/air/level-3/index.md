# Level 3: Air pushes

*Air and fluids · level 3 of 8 · rough age 9-10 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

Air is heavy stuff, and all the air above us presses down and in on everything. This level weighs air, holds water up
with a card, and explains straws, suction cups and the crisp packet that puffs up on a mountain. Then it works out how
little the air pushes on Mars.
