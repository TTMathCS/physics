# Level 8: A planet's shield

*Electricity and magnetism · level 8 of 8 · rough age 17 and beyond (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

The last level puts the fields to work on a planetary scale. Charged particles spiral in magnetic fields; the solar wind
pushes on every planet; Earth's field holds it off ten Earth radii out, while Mars, with no field, takes the wind
almost on its upper air. You will see why a planet's dynamo can die, how fast Mars is losing its air today, and whether
a magnet could shield astronauts on the way there.
