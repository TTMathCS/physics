# Electricity and magnetism

Every rover, radio and heater runs on electricity, and a magnetic field shields Earth in a way Mars's no longer
can. This topic starts with magnets and batteries and climbs to circuits, Ohm's law, fields, motors and generators, and
the story of a planet that lost its magnetic field and, with it, much of its air.
