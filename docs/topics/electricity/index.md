# Electricity and magnetism

Every rover, radio and heater runs on electricity, and Earth's magnetic field shields us in a way Mars's no longer
does. This topic starts with magnets and batteries and climbs to circuits, fields, motors and the story of a planet
that lost its magnetic field.

8 levels. The ages are only a rough guide for parents and teachers; anyone can start at any level.

| Level | Rough age | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Level 1: Magnets and batteries | 5-6 | What magnets stick to, a battery and a bulb |
| Level 2: Circuits | 7-8 | Complete circuits, switches, conductors and insulators |
| Level 3: Static and current | 9-10 | Static electricity, dust that clings to suits and panels |
| Level 4: Volts, amps, watts | 11-12 | Measuring a circuit, power from a solar panel |
| Level 5: Ohm's law | 12-13 | V = IR, series and parallel, resistance of wires |
| Level 6: Fields | 13-14 | Electric and magnetic fields, Earth's field and the remains of Mars's field in its crust |
| Level 7: Motors and generators | 15-16 | Electromagnetism, induction, the motors in a rover's wheels |
| Level 8: A planet's shield | 17 and beyond | Planetary dynamos, the solar wind stripping the air of Mars (MAVEN's results) |
