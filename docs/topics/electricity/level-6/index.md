# Level 6: Fields

*Electricity and magnetism · level 6 of 8 · rough age 13-14 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

Charges and magnets push and pull without touching, through a field that fills the space around them. This level
draws and measures electric and magnetic fields, uses Coulomb's law, and looks at the field round the Earth. Then it
turns to Mars, whose global field died billions of years ago but left magnetized rocks behind, and to the green aurora a
rover saw fill the Martian night sky.
