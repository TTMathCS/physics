# Level 4: Volts, amps, watts

*Electricity and magnetism · level 4 of 8 · rough age 11-12 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

Three numbers describe a circuit: how hard the charge is pushed (volts), how much flows each second (amps), and how
fast energy is delivered (watts). This level measures all three with meters, finds the power of a solar panel, and works
out how long the same panel takes to charge a battery on Mars and on Earth.
