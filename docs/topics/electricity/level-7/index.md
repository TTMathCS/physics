# Level 7: Motors and generators

*Electricity and magnetism · level 7 of 8 · rough age 15-16 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

A current makes a magnetic field, and a magnetic field pushes on a current. Put those together and you have a motor.
Turn it round, moving a wire through a field to drive a current, and you have a generator. This level works through
both with numbers, adds the transformer, and ends with the motors that drive a rover's wheels and spun a helicopter's
rotors on Mars.
