# Level 5: Ohm's law

*Electricity and magnetism · level 5 of 8 · rough age 12-13 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

Resistance is how hard a part makes it for current to flow. Ohm's law, $V = IR$, ties it to voltage and current. This
level uses it to solve series and parallel circuits, finds the resistance of a wire from its length and thickness, and
shows that copper wires on a cold Mars night carry current more easily than on Earth.
