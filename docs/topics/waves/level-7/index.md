# Level 7: Lenses and telescopes

*Waves, sound and light · level 7 of 8 · rough age 15-16 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

This level bends light on purpose. You use Snell's law and total internal reflection, then lenses: focal length, ray
diagrams and the lens equation. You build a telescope from two lenses, point it at Mars, and finish by working out how
the camera on a Mars orbiter photographed the Earth and Moon from 142 million km away.
