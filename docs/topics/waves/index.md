# Waves, sound and light

On Mars, sound travels at about 240 m/s instead of Earth's 340, high notes fade fast, and the sunset glows blue.
This topic starts with echoes and rainbows and climbs to vibrations, wave speed, colour, lenses and telescopes, and the
spectra a rover reads from a rock it has zapped with a laser.
