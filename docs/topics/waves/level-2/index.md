# Level 2: Light and shadow

*Waves, sound and light · level 2 of 8 · rough age 7-8 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

Where does light come from, and how does it get to your eyes? This level finds the things that give out light, shows
that light travels in straight lines, makes shadows bigger and smaller, and plays with mirrors. At the end you stand in
the sunlight of Mars, under a smaller, dimmer Sun.

| Lesson | You will be able to |
| --- | --- |
| [Light sources](01-light-sources.md) | Sort things that give out light from things that only reflect it |
| [Light travels in straight lines](02-light-travels-in-straight-lines.md) | Show that light goes in straight lines |
| [Shadows](03-shadows.md) | Explain how a shadow forms, and make one bigger or smaller |
| [Mirrors](04-mirrors.md) | Use a mirror to bounce light, and explain mirror writing |
| [How we see](05-how-we-see.md) | Trace the path of light from a lamp, to a book, to your eye |
| [Sunlight on Mars](06-sunlight-on-mars.md) | Compare the Sun and sunlight on Mars with Earth's |
