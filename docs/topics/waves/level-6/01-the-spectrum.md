# The spectrum

*Waves, sound and light · level 6, lesson 1 of 6*

## A question to start

Isaac Newton passed sunlight through a glass prism and saw it spread into a band of colours. What makes one colour
different from another?

## The idea

Light is a wave, and each **colour** is light of a different **wavelength**. The band of colours from a prism is the
**visible spectrum**. Its wavelengths are tiny, so we measure them in **nanometres** (nm): $1 \text{ nm} = 10^{-9}$ m.

| Colour | Wavelength, about |
| --- | --- |
| Red | 620 to 750 nm |
| Orange | 590 to 620 nm |
| Yellow | 570 to 590 nm |
| Green | 495 to 570 nm |
| Blue | 450 to 495 nm |
| Violet | 380 to 450 nm |

A prism splits white light because glass slows short wavelengths a little **more** than long ones. Violet bends most,
red least. This spreading is called **dispersion**.

The frequency of each colour follows from $c = f\lambda$.

## Worked examples

**1. Red light at 700 nm.** $f = c/\lambda = 3.00\times10^8/700\times10^{-9} = 4.3\times10^{14}$ Hz.

**2. Violet at 400 nm.** $f = 3.00\times10^8/400\times10^{-9} = 7.5\times10^{14}$ Hz.

**3. Mixing back.** Pass the spread colours through a second prism, turned the other way, and they mix back into white
light.

## Try it

1. Which colour has the longest wavelength?
2. Find the frequency of green light at 530 nm.
3. Why does a prism split white light into colours?
4. Light has a frequency of $6.0\times10^{14}$ Hz. Find its wavelength and colour.

??? note "Answers"
    1. Red.
    2. $3.00\times10^8/530\times10^{-9} = 5.7\times10^{14}$ Hz.
    3. Glass slows each wavelength by a slightly different amount, so each colour bends by a different angle.
    4. $\lambda = 3.00\times10^8/6.0\times10^{14} = 500$ nm: blue-green.

## Common mistake

**"The prism adds the colours."** The colours were already in the white light. The prism only separates them.
