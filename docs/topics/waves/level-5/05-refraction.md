# Refraction

*Waves, sound and light · level 5, lesson 5 of 6*

## A question to start

A straw standing in a glass of water looks bent at the surface. The straw is straight. What is bending?

## The idea

Light is slower in water and glass than in air. When light crosses from one into the other at an angle, it changes
direction. This is **refraction**.

- Going into a **slower** medium (air → glass), light bends **towards** the normal.
- Going into a **faster** medium (glass → air), it bends **away** from the normal.
- Hitting the surface straight on, along the normal, it slows down but does not bend.

How much a material slows light is given by its **refractive index**:

$$n = \frac{c}{v}.$$

| Material | $n$ | Speed of light in it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Air | about 1.000 | $3.00\times10^8$ m/s |
| Water | 1.33 | $2.26\times10^8$ m/s |
| Glass | about 1.5 | $2.0\times10^8$ m/s |

The frequency does not change as the light crosses; the speed and the wavelength both drop.

## Worked examples

**1. Speed in water.** $v = c/n = 3.00\times10^8/1.33 = 2.26\times10^8$ m/s.

**2. The straw.** Light from the part of the straw under water bends as it leaves the water, so it seems to come from
higher up. The straw looks bent at the surface.

**3. Wavelength in glass.** Green light of 550 nm in air has, in glass with $n = 1.5$, a wavelength of $550/1.5 = 367$
nm.

## Try it

1. What is refraction?
2. A ray passes from water into air at an angle. Does it bend towards or away from the normal?
3. Light travels at $1.24\times10^8$ m/s in diamond. Find diamond's refractive index.
4. Why does a ray that hits glass straight on, along the normal, not bend?

??? note "Answers"
    1. The change of direction of a wave as it crosses into a medium where its speed is different.
    2. Away from the normal.
    3. $n = 3.00\times10^8/1.24\times10^8 = 2.42$.
    4. Every part of the wavefront slows down at the same moment, so it changes speed without turning.

## Common mistake

**"Refraction changes the frequency."** The frequency stays the same. Only the speed, the wavelength and (at an angle)
the direction change.
