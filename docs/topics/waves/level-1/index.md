# Level 1: Echoes and rainbows

*Waves, sound and light · level 1 of 8 · rough age 5-6 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

Sound and light are all around you. This first level makes sounds by shaking things, makes them loud and quiet, high and
low, and bounces them back as echoes. Then it splits sunlight into a rainbow. At the end you find out how sounds and
colours change on Mars, where the sunset is blue.

| Lesson | You will be able to |
| --- | --- |
| [Making sounds](01-making-sounds.md) | Show that sounds come from things shaking |
| [Loud and quiet](02-loud-and-quiet.md) | Make a sound louder or quieter |
| [High and low sounds](03-high-and-low-sounds.md) | Tell high sounds from low sounds, and make each one |
| [Echoes](04-echoes.md) | Say what an echo is and where you can hear one |
| [Rainbows](05-rainbows.md) | Name the colours of the rainbow and say what makes one |
| [Sounds and colours on Mars](06-sounds-and-colours-on-mars.md) | Say how sounds and sky colours are different on Mars |
