# Level 8: Spectra

*Waves, sound and light · level 8 of 8 · rough age 17 and beyond (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

The last level reads the fingerprints that atoms leave in light. You meet photons and energy levels, work out the
hydrogen lines from the Rydberg formula, and see how cool gas cuts dark lines into a spectrum. A diffraction grating
spreads the lines apart; a rover's laser makes rocks glow so their elements can be read from metres away; and the
Doppler shift of a line measures how fast things move.
