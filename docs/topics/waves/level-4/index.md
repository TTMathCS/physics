# Level 4: Sound on Mars

*Waves, sound and light · level 4 of 8 · rough age 11-12 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

In 2021, Perseverance's microphones gave us the first real recordings of sound on Mars. This level explains what they
found: sounds about 20 decibels quieter than on Earth, a speed of sound of about 240 m/s, two speeds for high and low
notes, and high notes that fade fast. Then it asks what talking on Mars would be like.
