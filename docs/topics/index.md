# Physics by topic

Each topic is a ladder of **8 levels**, and each level holds several full lessons. It starts with something you can
try with your hands and climbs, one level at a time, to the physics of the end of high school and a first step
beyond. Every topic keeps coming back to one question: what would this mean on Mars?

Every lesson has the same parts: a question to start, the idea explained with pictures, worked examples, questions
to try (answers hidden until you tap them), a common mistake, and where it leads.

For parents and teachers: level 1 suits most children from about 5, level 3 from about 9, level 5 from about 12,
level 7 from about 15, and level 8 is the bridge to university. These are rough guides; curious readers ignore them.
