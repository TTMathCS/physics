# Level 4: Inside the atom

*Radiation and the atom · level 4 of 8 · rough age 11-12 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

An atom is not a solid ball. It has a tiny heavy centre, the nucleus, made of protons and neutrons, with electrons
around it. This level opens the atom up: what the nucleus is made of, why the number of protons decides the element,
and how atoms of one element can still differ. It ends with what different kinds of argon and water tell us about the
air Mars has lost.
