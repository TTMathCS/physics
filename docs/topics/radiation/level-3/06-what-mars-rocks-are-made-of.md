# What Mars rocks are made of

*Radiation and the atom · level 3, lesson 6 of 6*

## A question to start

Rovers on Mars carry instruments that can measure exactly which elements are in a rock or a patch of soil. What have
they found?

## The idea

Rocks are made mostly of compounds of a few elements, joined with **oxygen**. Geologists list them as **oxides**: silica
(silicon and oxygen), iron oxide, magnesium oxide and so on.

Mars Pathfinder's little rover, Sojourner, measured the soil where it landed in 1997. Compared with the rock of Earth's continents, Mars
soil has **less silica** and **about three times as much iron oxide**:

| Out of every 100 g (Mars soil, Earth's continental crust) | Mars | Earth |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Silica (silicon and oxygen) | 43.8 g | 60.2 g |
| Iron oxide | 17.5 g | 6.05 g |
| Aluminium oxide | 10.1 g | 15.2 g |
| Magnesium oxide | 8.6 g | 3.1 g |
| Calcium oxide | 5.3 g | 5.5 g |

Rock with less silica and more iron and magnesium is typical of **basalt**, the dark rock that forms from runny lava, as
in Hawaii and Iceland. Mars is covered in it, and its iron is what rusts and turns Mars red.

## Worked examples

**1. Three times the iron.** $17.5 \div 6.05 \approx 2.9$: nearly three times as much iron oxide in Mars soil.

**2. The most common element.** Oxygen. Pathfinder found that about 43 g in every 100 g of Mars soil is oxygen, joined to
the other elements.

**3. A rover's lab.** Curiosity found that Mars soil holds crystals of olivine, feldspar and pyroxene, much like the
weathered volcanic soil of Hawaii.

## Try it

1. What element is joined to most of the others in rock?
2. Which has more iron oxide: Mars soil or Earth's continents? About how many times as much?
3. Which has more silica?
4. Why do geologists think Mars's surface came largely from volcanoes?

??? note "Answers"
    1. Oxygen.
    2. Mars soil, about three times as much.
    3. Earth's continental crust.
    4. Its rock has less silica and more iron and magnesium, like basalt lava.

## Common mistake

**"Mars is made of different elements from Earth."** Both are made of the same elements. The amounts are different:
Mars has more iron near its surface and less silica.
