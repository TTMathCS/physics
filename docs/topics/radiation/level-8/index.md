# Level 8: Nuclear physics

*Radiation and the atom · level 8 of 8 · rough age 17+ (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

Where does nuclear energy come from? This level follows it to its source: mass turning into energy. It weighs nuclei
to find their binding energy, explains why both splitting and joining nuclei release energy, and puts the decay law to
work as a clock: one that tells the age of a Mars rock, and another that tells how long it has lain at the surface.

| Lesson | You will be able to |
| --- | --- |
| [Mass and energy](01-mass-and-energy.md) | Use $E = mc^2$ to turn mass into energy |
| [Binding energy](02-binding-energy.md) | Work out a nucleus's binding energy from its mass |
| [Fission and fusion](03-fission-and-fusion.md) | Explain from the binding energy curve why both release energy |
| [The decay law](04-the-decay-law.md) | Use half-lives, decay constants and activity |
| [Dating Mars rocks](05-dating-mars-rocks.md) | Work out a rock's age from its potassium and argon |
| [Exposure ages](06-exposure-ages.md) | Work out how long a rock has lain near the surface |
