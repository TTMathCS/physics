# Level 6: Doses

*Radiation and the atom · level 6 of 8 · rough age 13-14 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

How much radiation is too much? This level measures it: first as energy absorbed, in grays, then as harm, in sieverts.
It climbs the ladder of everyday doses, from a dental X-ray to a year in space, and then adds up the dose for a whole
trip to Mars and back.
