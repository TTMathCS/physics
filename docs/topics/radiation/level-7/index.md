# Level 7: Shielding

*Radiation and the atom · level 7 of 8 · rough age 15-16 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

A shield works by putting matter in the way. This level works out how much: how each kind of radiation loses its
energy, how a gamma beam halves with each layer, and why the mass over each square centimetre matters more than the
material. It ends with the question at the heart of this topic: how deep under Mars soil should a home be?
