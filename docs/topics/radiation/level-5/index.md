# Level 5: Kinds of radiation

*Radiation and the atom · level 5 of 8 · rough age 12-13 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

Some nuclei are unstable. Sooner or later they break down and throw out radiation: alpha, beta or gamma. From space
comes another kind, cosmic rays. This level meets each one, what it is made of and what stops it. It ends with the
radiation a crew would meet on the way to Mars.
