# Level 4: Into space and down to Mars

*Rockets and landing · level 4 of 8 · rough age 11-12 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

In this level you follow a real trip. You find where space begins and why getting to orbit is about speed, not height,
then fly the seven months to Mars and live through the "seven minutes of terror" of a landing: heat shield, a parachute
opening faster than sound, and rocket engines, down to the sky crane that set Perseverance on the ground.

| Lesson | You will be able to |
| --- | --- |
| [Where space begins](01-where-space-begins.md) | Say where space begins, and how the air thins with height on Earth and on Mars |
| [Orbit is speed](02-orbit-is-speed.md) | Explain why reaching orbit takes far more speed than reaching space, and compare Earth and Mars |
| [The trip to Mars](03-the-trip-to-mars.md) | Describe the steps of a trip to Mars, how long it takes and why |
| [Seven minutes](04-seven-minutes.md) | Follow every step of a Mars landing, with its speeds and times |
| [Parachutes in thin air](05-parachutes-in-thin-air.md) | Explain why Mars parachutes are so big, open so fast, and still cannot finish the job |
| [Landing on rockets](06-landing-on-rockets.md) | Compare legs, airbags and the sky crane, and explain why each was chosen |
