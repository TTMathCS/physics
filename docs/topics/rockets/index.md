# Rockets and landing

A rocket is the only machine that can push itself through empty space. This topic starts with a balloon whizzing
across a room and climbs to Newton's laws, momentum, the rocket equation and staging, then to the hardest part of any
Mars trip: getting down through air too thin for parachutes alone, from 20,000 km/h to a gentle touch on the ground.
