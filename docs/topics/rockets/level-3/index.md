# Level 3: Forces on a rocket

*Rockets and landing · level 3 of 8 · rough age 9-10 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

In this level you put numbers on the pushes. You measure forces in newtons, compare a rocket's thrust with its weight,
meet drag and the moment of greatest strain on a rocket, work out how quickly a rocket speeds up, see why rockets come
in stages, and find out how a heat shield survives coming back through the air.

| Lesson | You will be able to |
| --- | --- |
| [Forces in newtons](01-forces-in-newtons.md) | Measure forces in newtons, and work out weight on Earth and on Mars |
| [Thrust and weight](02-thrust-and-weight.md) | Find the leftover force that lifts a rocket from its thrust and weight |
| [Drag](03-drag.md) | Explain drag on a rocket, and why it is worst about a minute after launch |
| [Speeding up](04-speeding-up.md) | Work out how fast a rocket gains speed, and why it gets quicker as it climbs |
| [Stages](05-stages.md) | Explain why dropping empty stages lets a rocket go much faster |
| [Heat shields](06-heat-shields.md) | Explain why coming back from space is so hot, and how heat shields protect a ship |
