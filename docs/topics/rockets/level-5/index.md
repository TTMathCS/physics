# Level 5: Newton's laws for rockets

*Rockets and landing · level 5 of 8 · rough age 12-13 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

This level turns the ideas of levels 1 to 4 into equations. You use Newton's second law, $F = ma$, for rockets, landers
and rovers; compare thrust with weight on two planets; meet momentum and impulse, which explain soft landings; find a
rocket's thrust from the speed and flow of its exhaust; and put a number on drag.

| Lesson | You will be able to |
| --- | --- |
| [F = ma](01-f-equals-ma.md) | State Newton's three laws and use $F_\text{net} = ma$ with free-body diagrams |
| [Thrust-to-weight ratio](02-thrust-to-weight.md) | Find a rocket's starting acceleration from its thrust-to-weight ratio, on Earth and on Mars |
| [Momentum](03-momentum.md) | Use $p = mv$, and compare the momentum of rovers, rockets and exhaust |
| [Impulse](04-impulse.md) | Use $F\,\Delta t = \Delta p$ to find landing forces, and explain why a longer stop is gentler |
| [Exhaust speed](05-exhaust-speed.md) | Use thrust $= \dot m\, v_e$ to link an engine's thrust, propellant flow and exhaust speed |
| [Drag and speed](06-drag-and-speed.md) | Use $F = \tfrac12\rho v^2 C_d A$ and dynamic pressure to find max Q |
