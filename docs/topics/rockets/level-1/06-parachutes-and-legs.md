# Parachutes and legs

*Rockets and landing · level 1, lesson 6 of 6*

## A question to start

A spacecraft arrives at Mars going about 20,000 km an hour, much faster than a bullet. It has to touch the ground
going about as fast as you walk. How can it lose all that speed?

## The idea

It does it in steps, using a different trick for each one.

1. **Heat shield.** The ship hits the top of the air so fast that the air in front of it gets hotter than a furnace.
   A thick **heat shield** on the bottom takes the heat, and rubbing through the air slows the ship down a lot.
2. **Parachute.** When it is slow enough, a huge parachute pops out. On Mars the air is very thin, so the parachute
   has to be enormous, and still it cannot slow the ship enough on its own.
3. **Rockets.** Then rocket engines fire downwards, pushing up against the fall, to slow it down more.
4. **The touch.** Last, something soft for the landing: squashy **legs**, big bouncy **airbags**, or, for the
   Perseverance rover, a **sky crane** that hovered on rockets and lowered the rover to the ground on cables.

All of this takes about **seven minutes**. It happens so fast, and Mars is so far away, that nobody on Earth can steer
it. The ship does it all by itself.

## Worked examples

**1. Airbags.** In 1997 the Pathfinder lander, and in 2004 the rovers Spirit and Opportunity, landed inside big
balloons. They bounced across the ground, more than a dozen times, before rolling to a stop.

**2. Legs.** In 2008 the Phoenix lander came down on rocket engines and three legs, near the north pole of Mars.

## Try it

1. What are the four steps a ship uses to slow down at Mars?
2. Why does a heat shield get so hot?
3. Why can't a parachute alone land a ship on Mars?
4. Name a lander that used airbags, and one that used a sky crane.

??? note "Answers"
    1. Heat shield, parachute, rocket engines, and legs, airbags or a sky crane.
    2. The ship is going so fast that it squashes and heats the air in front of it.
    3. The air is so thin that even a huge parachute leaves the ship going too fast.
    4. Airbags: Pathfinder, Spirit or Opportunity. Sky crane: Perseverance (or Curiosity).

## Common mistake

**"Landing on Mars is like landing on the Moon."** The Moon has no air, so landers there use only rockets. Mars has
just enough air to burn a ship that comes in too fast, but not enough to slow it with parachutes alone. It needs every
trick.
