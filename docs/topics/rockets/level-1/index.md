# Level 1: Push and go

*Rockets and landing · level 1 of 8 · rough age 5-6 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

Things start moving when they are pushed. In this level you push toy cars, launch a balloon rocket across the room,
race fast and slow things, and send a rocket up into the sky. Then comes the hard part, coming back down: how to land
softly, with parachutes, legs and rockets, on Earth and on Mars.

| Lesson | You will be able to |
| --- | --- |
| [Pushes make things go](01-pushes-make-things-go.md) | Say that a push or a pull starts things moving, stops them and turns them |
| [The balloon rocket](02-the-balloon-rocket.md) | Make a balloon rocket and explain that air rushing out one way pushes the balloon the other way |
| [Fast and slow](03-fast-and-slow.md) | Compare speeds, and say that a bigger push makes a bigger change |
| [Up into the sky](04-up-into-the-sky.md) | Explain that a rocket must push down harder than its weight to go up |
| [Coming down gently](05-coming-down-gently.md) | Explain why landing softly means slowing down slowly |
| [Parachutes and legs](06-parachutes-and-legs.md) | Name the ways landers slow down and land on Mars: heat shields, parachutes, rockets, legs and airbags |
