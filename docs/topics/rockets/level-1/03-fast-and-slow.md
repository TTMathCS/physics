# Fast and slow

*Rockets and landing · level 1, lesson 3 of 6*

## A question to start

A snail, a bicycle, a car, an aeroplane and a rocket. Put them in order from slowest to fastest. How fast does a rocket
have to go to get to space?

## The idea

Speed is how far something goes in a certain time. A fast thing goes a long way in a short time; a slow thing goes a
little way.

| Thing | About how far it goes in one second |
| --- | --- |
| A snail | 1 millimetre (the width of a grain of sand) |
| You, walking | 1 metre (one big step) |
| A bicycle | 5 metres |
| A car on the motorway | 30 metres |
| A jet plane | 250 metres |
| A rocket going round the Earth | 8,000 metres (8 kilometres) |
| A ship on its way to Mars (going round the Sun) | about 30,000 metres |

A rocket in orbit goes about **8 km every second**: from one side of a city to the other in a couple of seconds. It
goes right round the whole Earth in an hour and a half.

To get that fast, a rocket has to keep pushing for a long time. A big push for a short time makes a small change; a
big push for a long time makes a huge change. A rocket's engines push for about eight to ten minutes to reach orbit.

## Worked examples

**1. Racing toy cars.** Push two toy cars, one gently and one hard. The hard push makes the faster car.

**2. A heavy and a light car.** Give the same push to a toy truck full of blocks and an empty one. The empty one goes
faster. Heavy things need a bigger push to get going.

## Try it

1. Which is faster, a bicycle or a jet plane?
2. About how far does a rocket in orbit go in one second?
3. You push an empty cart and a full cart the same. Which goes faster?
4. How can a rocket get so fast?

??? note "Answers"
    1. The jet plane, by a lot.
    2. About 8 km.
    3. The empty cart.
    4. Its engines keep pushing hard for many minutes.

## Common mistake

**"Rockets go fast because they are big."** Big rockets are big because they carry lots of fuel, so they can keep
pushing for a long time. A tiny rocket with a lot of fuel could go fast too.
