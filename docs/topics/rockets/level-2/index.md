# Level 2: Action and reaction

*Rockets and landing · level 2 of 8 · rough age 7-8 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

Every push has a push back. In this level you feel it with your hands, ride it on a skateboard, and find out why it
lets rockets work in empty space. You learn why a rocket must carry its own oxygen, build a water rocket, and name the
parts of a real rocket and what each one does.

| Lesson | You will be able to |
| --- | --- |
| [Push back](01-push-back.md) | Say that when you push something, it pushes back on you just as hard |
| [The skateboard and the ball](02-skateboard-and-ball.md) | Explain why throwing a heavy ball from a skateboard rolls you backwards |
| [No air needed](03-no-air-needed.md) | Explain why rockets work in space, where there is nothing to push against |
| [Fuel and oxygen](04-fuel-and-oxygen.md) | Say why a rocket carries oxygen as well as fuel, and how oxygen could be made on Mars |
| [The water rocket](05-water-rocket.md) | Build and test a water rocket, and say why water works better than air alone |
| [The parts of a rocket](06-parts-of-a-rocket.md) | Name the parts of a rocket and say what each part is for |
