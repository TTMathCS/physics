# Level 8: Thermodynamics

*Heat and matter · level 8 of 8 · rough age 17 and beyond (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

The last level is the laws behind every engine, fridge and climate. You meet the first law, the energy balance of a gas,
and the second law, which says which way heat can flow and introduces entropy. You use them on heat pumps for a Mars
home and on engine cycles, then turn to the planet itself: why Mars's carbon dioxide air warms it so little, and what
drives its seasons.

| Lesson | You will be able to |
| --- | --- |
| [The first law](01-the-first-law.md) | Use $\Delta U = Q - W$, and the work $p\Delta V$ a gas does |
| [The second law](02-the-second-law.md) | Use $\Delta S = Q/T$ to show which way heat flows |
| [Heat pumps](03-heat-pumps.md) | Find the best possible COP of a heat pump on Mars and on Earth |
| [Engines and cycles](04-engines-and-cycles.md) | Find the work of a cycle as the area on a $p$–$V$ diagram |
| [The greenhouse effect on Mars](05-the-greenhouse-effect-on-mars.md) | Find a planet's black-body temperature, and the warming its air adds |
| [The climate of Mars](06-the-climate-of-mars.md) | Explain Mars's seasons, its carbon dioxide cycle and its dust storms |
