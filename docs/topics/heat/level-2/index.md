# Level 2: Solid, liquid, gas

*Heat and matter · level 2 of 8 · rough age 7-8 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

Water can be ice, water or steam. In this level you meet the three states of matter and the changes between them:
melting, freezing, boiling, evaporation and condensation. Then you find the ice on Mars, including ice made of frozen
air.
