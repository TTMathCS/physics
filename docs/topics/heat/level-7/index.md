# Level 7: Insulating a home

*Heat and matter · level 7 of 8 · rough age 15-16 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

A home on Mars sits in air about 80 °C colder than its rooms. This level works out how fast heat leaks through walls,
layers and windows, and by radiation from surfaces. Then it covers a habitat in Martian soil and builds a heating
budget, which shows that a well-sealed Mars home may need cooling as much as heating.
