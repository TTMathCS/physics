# Level 3: Heat on the move

*Heat and matter · level 3 of 8 · rough age 9-10 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

Heat always moves from hot to cold, and it has three ways to travel: through solids by conduction, carried by moving
air and water by convection, and across empty space by radiation. This level takes each in turn, then puts all three
together to keep a rover warm on a Mars night.
