# Level 6: Phase changes

*Heat and matter · level 6 of 8 · rough age 13-14 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

Melting and boiling take energy without changing the temperature. In this level you put numbers on that, the latent
heat, and find the energy to turn Mars ice into drinking water. Then you see why the boiling point depends on pressure,
what the triple point is, how to read a phase diagram, and why liquid water is so rare on Mars today.

| [Boiling point and pressure](03-boiling-point-and-pressure.md) | Use vapour pressure to find the boiling point on a mountain and on Mars |
