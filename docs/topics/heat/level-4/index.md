# Level 4: Temperature, measured

*Heat and matter · level 4 of 8 · rough age 11-12 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

Now the numbers. You meet the kelvin scale and absolute zero, the difference between heat and temperature, and the
formula $Q = mc\Delta T$ for how much energy it takes to warm something up. Then you use it to see why things grow
when they warm, and why a Mars night gets so cold so fast.

| Lesson | You will be able to |
| --- | --- |
| [Celsius and kelvin](01-celsius-and-kelvin.md) | Convert between °C, K and °F, and explain absolute zero |
| [Heat and temperature](02-heat-and-temperature.md) | Explain why a hot spark holds less heat than a warm bath |
| [Specific heat capacity](03-specific-heat-capacity.md) | Use $Q = mc\Delta T$, and compare warming air on Mars and on Earth |
| [Heating water](04-heating-water.md) | Use $Q = Pt$ to find how long heating takes, and size a hot-water store |
| [Thermal expansion](05-thermal-expansion.md) | Use $\Delta L = \alpha L \Delta T$ for a beam through a Mars day and night |
| [A Mars night](06-a-mars-night.md) | Explain why Mars's temperature swings so far between day and night |
