# Heat and temperature

*Heat and matter · level 4, lesson 2 of 6*

## A question to start

The sparks from a sparkler are over 1,000 °C, yet they land on your hand without burning it. A bath at 40 °C is much
cooler. Which holds more heat?

## The idea

**Temperature** and **heat** are not the same thing.

- **Temperature** tells you how fast the particles are jiggling on average. It is measured in °C or K.
- **Thermal energy** is the total energy of all that jiggling, so it depends on **how much stuff** there is as well. It
  is measured in joules (J).
- **Heat** is thermal energy on the move, from hotter to colder.

A spark is very hot but tiny: it holds very little thermal energy, and it cools the moment it touches you. A bath is
only warm but holds a huge amount.

## Worked examples

**1. Cup and bucket.** A cup and a bucket of water, both at 80 °C, have the same temperature. The bucket holds far more
thermal energy and would melt far more ice.

**2. Mixing.** Pour the cup into the bucket: the temperature stays 80 °C, but the thermal energy adds up.

**3. Mars air.** At the same temperature, a cubic metre of Mars air holds about a ninetieth of the thermal energy of
Earth air, because there is so little of it. That is why it cannot keep the ground warm at night.

## Try it

1. What does temperature tell you about the particles?
2. Which has more thermal energy, a lake at 10 °C or a cup of tea at 70 °C?
3. Why does a spark from a sparkler not burn you?
4. Two pans of water are both at 50 °C, one holding 1 kg and one 2 kg. Which takes longer to cool to 20 °C on the same
   cooker turned off? Why?

??? note "Answers"
    1. How fast they are moving on average.
    2. The lake: it is far bigger.
    3. It holds so little thermal energy that it cools almost at once.
    4. The 2 kg pan, if both lose heat at about the same rate: it has twice as much thermal energy to lose.

## Common mistake

**"Hotter always means more heat."** Temperature is about each particle; thermal energy is about all of them. A small
hot thing can hold less than a big warm one.
