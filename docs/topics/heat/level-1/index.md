# Level 1: Hot and cold

*Heat and matter · level 1 of 8 · rough age 5-6 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

This first level is about the hot and cold things all around you: how you can tell, how a thermometer tells you, what
the Sun does, and how coats and blankets keep you warm. At the end you find out just how cold Mars is, and how its
rovers keep warm.

| Lesson | You will be able to |
| --- | --- |
| [Hot and cold](01-hot-and-cold.md) | Sort things into hot, warm, cool and cold |
| [Thermometers](02-thermometers.md) | Read a thermometer in degrees Celsius |
| [The Sun warms things](03-the-sun-warms.md) | Say why sunny places are warmer than shady ones |
| [Keeping warm](04-keeping-warm.md) | Explain how coats and blankets keep warmth in |
| [Hot things cool down](05-hot-things-cool-down.md) | Say what happens to a hot drink or a cold ice lolly left out |
| [Cold on Mars](06-cold-on-mars.md) | Compare how cold Mars is with Earth |
