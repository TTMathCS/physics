# Level 2: Weight and pull

*Gravity and orbits · level 2 of 8 · rough age 7-8 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

In level 1 you saw that everything falls. In this level you measure the pull: weight. You learn the difference
between how much stuff something has and how hard a planet pulls on it, work out your weight on Mars and the Moon,
find the balancing point of odd shapes, and follow Newton's famous thought experiment of a cannon on a mountain. It
ends with how gravity and a tilted spin give us days, years and seasons, on Earth and on Mars.

| Lesson | You will be able to |
| --- | --- |
| [What weight is](01-what-weight-is.md) | Say that weight is the pull of gravity on a thing, and measure it with a spring or a scale |
| [Mass and weight](02-mass-and-weight.md) | Tell mass (how much stuff) from weight (how hard it is pulled), and say which changes on Mars |
| [Weight on other worlds](03-weight-on-other-worlds.md) | Work out what you would weigh on Mars, the Moon and Jupiter |
| [The balancing point](04-balancing-point.md) | Find the centre of mass of a ruler and odd shapes, and say why things tip over |
| [Newton's cannon](05-newtons-cannon.md) | Explain how throwing fast enough sideways turns a fall into an orbit |
| [Days, years and seasons](06-days-years-seasons.md) | Explain a day, a year and the seasons, and compare them on Mars and Earth |
