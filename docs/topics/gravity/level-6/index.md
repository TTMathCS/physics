# Level 6: Newton's law of gravity

*Gravity and orbits · level 6 of 8 · rough age 13-14 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

This level puts the whole of gravity into one equation, $F = GMm/r^2$, and gets everything else out of it: the 3.71 m/s²
of Mars from its mass and size, the speed and period of any circular orbit, Kepler's third law, the special orbit
where a satellite hangs over one spot of Mars, and finally how to weigh a planet by watching its moon.

| Lesson | You will be able to |
| --- | --- |
| [The law of gravity](01-the-law-of-gravity.md) | Use $F = GMm/r^2$ for planets, moons and people, and say how $G$ was measured |
| [g from a planet's size and mass](02-g-from-a-planet.md) | Derive $g = GM/R^2$, get 3.71 m/s² for Mars, and compare planets by ratios |
| [Orbital speed](03-orbital-speed.md) | Derive $v = \sqrt{GM/r}$ and find the speed and period of an orbit round Mars or Earth |
| [Kepler's third law](04-keplers-third-law.md) | Derive $T^2 = 4\pi^2 r^3 / GM$ and use it for planets and moons |
| [Areostationary orbit](05-areostationary-orbit.md) | Find the orbit that keeps pace with the spin of Mars, and say what it is for |
| [Weighing planets](06-weighing-planets.md) | Find the mass of Mars from Phobos's orbit, and the mass of the Sun from Earth's |
