# Level 5: Equations of falling

*Gravity and orbits · level 5 of 8 · rough age 12-13 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

In level 3 you found falling patterns from tables. In this level they become equations you can use for any height,
any time and any planet. You derive $s = \tfrac12 gt^2$ and $v^2 = 2gs$, split a throw into across and up-and-down,
work out a jump on Mars from your own jump at home, find terminal velocity from a balance of forces, and see how $g$
changes as you climb.

| Lesson | You will be able to |
| --- | --- |
| [Distance fallen](01-distance-fallen.md) | Derive and use $s = \tfrac12 gt^2$, and compare fall times on Earth and Mars |
| [The speed of a fall](02-speed-of-a-fall.md) | Use $v = gt$ and $v^2 = 2gs$ to find landing speeds, and match a fall on Mars to one on Earth |
| [Projectiles](03-projectiles.md) | Split a throw into across and up-and-down, and find its time, height and range |
| [The Mars jump, worked out](04-the-mars-jump.md) | Find your take-off speed from a jump at home, and work out the same jump on Mars |
| [Terminal velocity](05-terminal-velocity.md) | Balance weight and drag to find a falling speed, and see why Mars falls are so fast |
| [Gravity with height](06-gravity-with-height.md) | Use $g = g_0 R^2/(R+h)^2$ and its short form for small heights |
